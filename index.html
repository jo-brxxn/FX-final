❓ How FX Analyst Pro works

The score
Every indicator (CPI, NFP, PMI …) carries a bias: ▲ Bullish +1, ◆ Neutral 0, ▼ Bearish −1. A ⭐ star adds +0.5. Indicators with a Core twin (e.g. CPI + Core CPI) count ±0.5 each so the theme isn't double-weighted. Most biases update automatically from calendar releases (beat/miss vs. forecast); manual clicks stick only until the next release. Every release is also checked for a 2-period momentum trend (bullish if the actual improved on both the previous and the release before that, bearish if it worsened both times, otherwise no effect). If a release has no forecast to compare against, there's nothing to wait for: as soon as actual differs from previous (just 1 step, no confirmed trend needed yet), that direction sets the bias immediately, counted at ±0.5 - exactly like a beat/miss, just at half strength since it's a weaker signal. If the full 2-step trend ALSO confirms (2 consecutive moves the same way), that adds a further ±1 on top (additive, not a replacement) - so a confirmed trend totals ±1.5. If a forecast is available, the normal beat/miss bias (±1) stays in charge, and a confirmed 2-step trend can still add up to ±1 on top of it the same way (e.g. a beat plus two consecutive improving prints counts more than a beat alone). Half-weight indicators (CPI + Core CPI & co.) get half of the trend BONUS specifically (+0.5 instead of +1) - so with no forecast their confirmed-trend total is ±1 (0.5 base + 0.5 bonus), and with a forecast their bonus is ±0.5; the pair shares the full amount but each side triggers independently. Bond yields have their own trend: if the 2Y/10Y yield is higher than 25 days ago it adds +0.5 (lower: −0.5) per maturity, likewise sharing ±1. Every indicator with trend data shows a 📈 chip - "Trend +1/−1" (or ±0.5/±1.5) when active, or a faded "Trend 0/2"/"1/2" showing how close it is to the 2-step criterion when not. Tap the chip to see the actual values behind the check. Non-FX assets (Gold, Oil, indices, BTC …) get the same trend on indicators mirrored from their linked currency - direction included, flipped when the card's derive rule is "inverse".
Card score = sum of its indicators. A card turns bullish at ≥ +2 and bearish at ≤ −2. Symbol score = sum of all cards; the symbol turns bullish at ≥ +3 / bearish at ≤ −3.
Pairs & set-ups
Pair score = base score − quote score + carry (e.g. EUR −5, USD +5 → EUR/USD −10). The Δ%-badge is the policy-rate differential: it adds ±0.5 to the pair score from ±0.5% and ±1 from ±1.5% — positive carry favours being long the pair. Tap/click the badge for a breakdown (both policy rates, the differential and the exact score effect). The Set-ups tab sorts all pairs into Bullish / Neutral / Bearish columns from that.
Central bank & COT
The Interest Rates card has two extra indicators. CB Tone: your own read of the central bank's rhetoric — set it ▲ Bullish for hawkish, ▼ Bearish for dovish (counts at half weight, like a bond yield). Next CB Move: your expected next decision — ▲ Bullish for hike, ▼ Bearish for cut, ◆ Neutral for hold (full weight). Both are manual and stick until you change them. COT net positioning counts ±0.5 once one side holds ≥60% of speculative positions; the week-over-week change counts ±1 only when the shift is at least 3 percentage points away from 0 — smaller moves count ±0.5. The 2Y/10Y Spread is display-only and counts 0 — a 10Y move would otherwise be double-counted, and curve steepening isn't a clean bullish/bearish signal on its own.
